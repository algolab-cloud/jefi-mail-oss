# Jefi Mail 0.9.0

Free and open source, with all your inboxes in one place.

- **Free, and open source.** Jefi Mail is now free software under the GPL. The trial and licence keys are gone: everything works, with nothing to buy. The source is at [github.com/algolab-cloud/jefi-mail-oss](https://github.com/algolab-cloud/jefi-mail-oss).
- **All Inboxes and All Starred.** With more than one account, see every inbox, or everything you've starred, in a single list.
- **Sign-in codes in one click.** A code from a sign-in email appears beside the subject, in the menu bar and on the notification, and copies in one click.
- **Tracking stays blocked.** Tracking pixels stay blocked even after you show a message's images, and the reader says how many it blocked. Messages can also be translated into your language.
- **Your shortcuts.** Keyboard shortcuts can be single keys, ⌘ shortcuts or off, and you can change each one in Settings.
- **A cleaner reader.** One message opens at a time, in full, and the toolbar is grouped. ⌘F finds text in the conversation.
- **One Keychain prompt.** Passwords and keys are kept in one encrypted vault, so macOS asks for Keychain access once, not once per account.

Every download is signed and notarized by Apple, and comes with a build attestation linking it to the public commit it was built from.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
