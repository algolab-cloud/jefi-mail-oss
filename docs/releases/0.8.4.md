# Jefi 0.8.4

Meeting links on your events, a clearer word when an update is ready, and a way to tell us what's broken or missing.

- **Meeting links.** Paste a Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, FaceTime, Slack or Discord link into a new field in the event editor and Jefi names the service as you type. The event then has a **Join** button, and so do invitations with a meeting link in their place or notes. Guests get the link in the invitation, and hear about it when it changes.
- **Updates you'll notice.** A new version shows as a small card at the foot of the sidebar in Mail, Calendar and Tasks, with **Restart now** (or **Download**) and **Later**.
- **Report a bug or request a feature.** A new button in the left rail opens a short form that goes straight to us. It includes your Jefi and macOS versions unless you switch that off, and never your mail or accounts.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
