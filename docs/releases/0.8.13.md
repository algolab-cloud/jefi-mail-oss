# Jefi Mail 0.8.13

A new name, and a task sheet you can read.

- **Jefi is now Jefi Mail.** The menu bar, Dock, Finder, window and Notifications all say Jefi Mail. Nothing moves: your mail, settings and accounts stay as they are, and the app updates in place.
- **A solid task sheet.** In Liquid Glass, the task list no longer shows through a task's details.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
