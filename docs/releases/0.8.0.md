# Jefi 0.8.0

Email that asks something of you can now become a task, kept with the conversation it came from.

- **Tasks.** A new app in the rail (⌘4) that works like an issue tracker. Each task has a status (Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Done or Canceled), a priority from Urgent to Low, a number like T-12, a project and any labels. The sidebar has views for Active, Today, Upcoming, Backlog, All tasks and Completed, and for each project and label. **Filter** narrows the list by priority, label or project. **Display** switches between a list and a board, and groups tasks by status, priority, project or due date. Click a task and its details open in a wide sheet beside the list, where each property is one click to change; J and K step through the list. **New task** (or C) opens a composer with all the properties. The "Add a task" line takes a task the way you'd say it: "Send the deck Friday 3pm", "Pay rent tomorrow" or "Renew passport in 2 weeks" sets the due date for you.
- **On your calendar, with reminders.** Tasks with a due date show in the calendar on a Tasks calendar of their own, and clicking one lets you mark it done or open it. They remind you like events: choose when in **Settings → Tasks** (at the due time, 5 to 30 minutes, an hour or a day before, or 9:00 on the day for tasks due on a date), or give a task its own reminder. Changing a task's due date moves it on the calendar and its reminder with it.
- **Make a task from an email.** Press **T**, or choose **Make task** in the reader's toolbar, a message's menu, or the list's right-click menu. The subject becomes the title and the next date the email mentions becomes the due date ("Can we do the review on Thursday at 3pm?"), and you can change either before adding it. Tasks made from a conversation show under its subject, where you can tick them off.
- **Suggested action items.** With AI turned on in Settings, **Suggest tasks with Claude** (or OpenAI or ChatGPT) in the Make task form lists what you've been asked to do in the conversation, with any deadlines. It uses your own key or Codex login, like Jefi's other AI features, and nothing is added until you choose it.
- **Tasks in the menu bar.** The menu bar panel lists what's overdue or due today, with a circle to tick each one off. Start quick add with **Task:** ("Task: Call Kenji Friday") to add a task instead of an event, or use **New task**.
- **On your Mac.** Tasks are stored with your calendar and notes, not in any account, and they're kept if you remove an account; their links then show what the email was.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
