# Jefi 0.7.0

Your calendar and notes now live next to your mail, and Jefi keeps you on time from the menu bar.

- **A calendar in Jefi.** Day, week, month and agenda views, with calendars that live on your Mac. One called Personal is there from the start, and you can add more, each with its own colour. Create and edit events in place, add a location, notes and guests, and set them to repeat. Nothing signs in to Google or Microsoft, and your calendar doesn't sync with any account.
- **Invitations by email.** Invitations from Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar show as a card in the message, with Accept, Maybe and Decline. Your answer goes back to the organizer by email, and the event lands in your calendar. Invite guests to your own events and Jefi sends the invitation, and any later update or cancellation, from your own address.
- **Dates in your mail.** Jefi spots dates and times in the message you're reading, on your Mac, and offers Add to calendar: a small form with the title, time and calendar filled in, and More options for the full editor.
- **Event reminders.** Choose default alerts for timed and all-day events in Settings, change them per event, and get reminded for every occurrence of a repeating event. A reminder shows as a notification and opens the event when you click it.
- **Notes linked to your mail.** A notes view that saves as you type. Add to notes in the reader puts a message into a new note, or onto the end of a recent one. Notes and events that came from a conversation show as chips under its subject, and a note's links open the email or event they point to, even reading sensibly after the email is gone. Notes stay on your Mac.
- **An app rail.** A slim rail on the left switches between Mail, Calendar, Notes and the Assistant (⌘1, ⌘2, ⌘3), with search, sync status, your account and Settings. Each app gets its own sidebar beside it, which you can resize or hide with ⌘\.
- **Jefi in the menu bar.** A menu bar item opens a panel on macOS's Liquid Glass with what's up next, today's events, a month view, pending invitations and recent mail and reminders, plus buttons for a new message, event or note. Type something like "Lunch with Nora Friday 1pm" into its quick add field and the event editor opens with it filled in. Closing the window now keeps Jefi running there, so mail and reminders still arrive. Settings let you show your unread count, the next event or nothing beside its icon, turn the item off, and hide the Dock icon while the window is closed.
- **Notifications, set up clearly.** A new step in setup, and a one-time sheet for existing users, asks for notification permission and explains why. Settings shows whether notifications are allowed and links to System Settings if not.
- **Catch-up across accounts.** With more than one account, the Assistant can go through unread mail in every Inbox, not just the current one. AI features stay optional: bring an Anthropic API key, or an OpenAI API key or your Codex CLI login.
- **A new look.** A new Jefi logo and app icon.
- **Tidier attachments.** Attachments show as compact cards of one size, with a tile for the file type, the name, type and size. Click to open; Quick Look and Save appear on hover.

Fixes:

- Attachment actions no longer blink when you move the pointer over them.
- Popovers and the event card no longer show dark corners.
- Calendar invitations no longer also appear as attachments.
- Durations such as "for 2 hours" are no longer offered as events, and Day view opens on the morning instead of midnight.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**. The menu bar panel uses Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later.
