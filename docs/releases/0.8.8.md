# Jefi 0.8.8

Mail that keeps up with you.

- **Faster reading.** Opening a conversation marks it read, and that used to set off a full sync of every folder. Read and star changes now go straight to the server on their own, and a conversation opens together with its messages in one step. Opening and moving through your mail with J and K takes about half the work it did.
- **Archive and move on.** Archiving or deleting shows the next conversation with its messages already in place.
- **Less redrawing.** When a sync brings nothing new to the screen, nothing is redrawn, and starring or labelling a conversation no longer redraws every message in it.
- **Lighter toasts.** Toasts slide in faster, and a new one doesn't redraw the others.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
