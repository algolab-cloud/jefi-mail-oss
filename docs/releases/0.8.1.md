# Jefi 0.8.1

A round of fixes for reading and tidying your mail.

- **Characters that read right.** Some senders, Slack among them, send text that Jefi decoded wrongly, so apostrophes and other characters showed as boxes or vanished from previews, search and the message itself. They now read as sent, and messages already affected download again by themselves.
- **No more crash on some conversations.** A conversation that sat in your Inbox and in another folder at the same time could close the window when opened.
- **Archive and delete keep your place.** The reader moves on to the next conversation instead of jumping back to the top, and E, #, S and U act on every conversation you've checked, not just the open one.
- **Snooze that behaves.** Unsnoozing sticks, and archiving or deleting a snoozed conversation ends the snooze so it doesn't come back.
- **Gmail labels that last.** A label you make on a Gmail account is a real Gmail label, so it stays after the next sync. Deleting labels is offered only where Jefi can actually delete them.
- **Accurate Inbox counts.** The badges in the sidebar, the account switcher and the Dock count your whole Inbox, however much of it is loaded.
- **Smaller things.** Enter creates a new label and steps through Find matches, plain-text replies fold their quoted history, list previews stop before "On … wrote:", long subjects wrap in the reader, and the list scrolls to follow J and K.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
