# Jefi 0.8.2

Fixes for writing mail and for the calendar.

- **Invitations that go out.** Save and send invites failed every time; it now sends from the account the event came from, or the one you're reading. Editing an invitation someone sent you no longer tries to send one of your own.
- **Cancelling tells the guests, if you want.** Deleting your own event with guests asks whether to let them know, and sends the cancellation when you choose to.
- **The event editor listens to Enter.** Enter saves, applies a typed time and adds a guest; ⌘↩ no longer creates the event twice, and Escape closes an open menu before the editor.
- **Times you can trust.** Events on the day the clocks change sit at the right time, a weekly event moved to another day repeats on that day, and repeat end dates read right in every time zone. Cancellations that arrive before their invitation are kept.
- **No more stuck sends.** Some Markdown made sending or saving a draft hang; links and code with _ or * in them now arrive intact.
- **Nothing you type goes missing.** The composer saves when you switch conversation or account, a half-written reply waits for you when you come back, and a double-click on Send sends once.
- **Undo that undoes.** Undo send now holds replies too, and Discard can be undone.
- **Recipients that behave.** Enter adds an address, a pasted "Name <address>" is one recipient, an address you typed but didn't confirm is still included, and ↑ and ↓ choose suggestions. Send refuses anything that isn't an email address and says which.
- **Replies and forwards done right.** Replies keep Bcc, an edited subject and their place in the thread; forwards carry the original date, To and Cc.
- **Failed sends you can fix.** The Outbox says why a message couldn't be sent, and Edit takes it back to Drafts.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
