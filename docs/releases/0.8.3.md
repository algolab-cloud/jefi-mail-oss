# Jefi 0.8.3

Faster everywhere, and a sweep of fixes for Settings, the Assistant, notes and tasks.

- **A much faster first sync.** Threading used to slow down with every message it added; now it doesn't, and each conversation is rebuilt once per batch rather than for every message. Ten thousand messages sync two to three times faster, and big Gmail accounts gain the most.
- **Lighter on your Mac.** Jefi now runs React's production build, and the mailbox no longer redraws itself for sync progress you can't see.
- **An Assistant that checks first.** It asks before archiving or changing many conversations, Enter sends, Stop really stops, and when AI isn't ready it says whether you need to turn it on, sign in or add a key.
- **Enter works everywhere.** Trusted domains, labels, API keys, the account wizard, the command palette, onboarding and new calendars all take Enter.
- **A wizard that doesn't hang.** A server that answers and then goes quiet is given 30 seconds, not five minutes, and you can cancel at any time.
- **Rules and Clean up behave.** Editing a rule no longer makes a copy of it, and Clean up's spinners and messages no longer get stuck.
- **Links that land.** jefi://settings/… scrolls to the section it names, and a search opened by a link at launch fills in once your mail arrives.
- **Sturdier startup.** A damaged settings or accounts file no longer leaves a black window or crashes Settings.
- **Tasks you can rely on.** A date for today stays today after noon, Make task keeps the title you typed, tasks made in Today or Upcoming stay in view, T-numbers are never reused, and "send it by Friday" in an email becomes the due date. Reminders follow a due date that gains or loses its time.
- **Room to write.** Long notes, task notes and messages open at the top and use the whole pane.
- **Big lists stay quick.** Searching or typing with thousands of tasks or notes is now instant.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
