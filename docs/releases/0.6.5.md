# Jefi 0.6.5

Clearer answers when something needs your attention.

- **Licence messages that help.** Entering a key now tells you exactly what's wrong and what to do: an unrecognised key asks you to copy the whole key from your purchase email, a key already active on three Macs tells you how to free one up, and a revoked key says so plainly. If a routine check removes your licence, Jefi explains why instead of quietly returning to the trial.
- **AI stays off when it's off.** With AI features turned off in Settings, Jefi sets up no connection to any AI provider at all.
- **Clearer AI errors.** An invalid API key, a rate limit, a network problem, or a signed-out or missing Codex CLI each gets its own plain explanation.
- **A lighter default model.** Summaries and reply drafts now use Claude Sonnet 5 by default, which handles them well at a lower cost. Opus is one click away under Settings › AI › Model.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
