# Jefi 0.8.5

Labels for every account, and a choice of who a message is from.

- **Labels on every account.** On iCloud, Outlook, Fastmail and other accounts without labels of their own, the labels you make are kept on your Mac: they stay after a sync or a restart, follow a message from folder to folder, work in search and rules, and can be renamed or deleted. Gmail keeps using Gmail's own labels.
- **Choose the From account.** With more than one account, the composer lets you pick who a message is from. A reply starts from the account its conversation belongs to, and a draft remembers the choice.
- **Undo that brings it all back.** Undo after Send and archive returns the conversation to where it was, as well as taking back the reply.
- **No false alarms.** A conversation you move back to the Inbox, or bring back with Undo, is no longer announced as new mail, and your rules don't act on it a second time.
- **Remote images stay blocked.** Some HTML mail could load remote images, the kind used to track when you read, before you allowed them: a message with a `<header>` element, or an image ahead of the page's head. They're now blocked until you choose to show them, in every message.
- **Sign-in you can back out of.** Signing in with Microsoft or Google has a Cancel button, closing the window cancels too, and sign-in problems are explained in plain words.
- **New mail, calmly.** A new-mail notice names who wrote the new message rather than everyone in the conversation, and the reader stays on what you're reading when mail arrives above it.
- **Small things.** "Task:" on its own in quick add opens a new task, and Manage accounts opens Settings at Accounts.
- **Tidier drafts on the server.** A reply draft's server copy keeps its place in the conversation for other mail apps, and an unchanged draft is no longer uploaded again with all its attachments.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
