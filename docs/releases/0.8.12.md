# Jefi 0.8.12

A calmer look, notes you can tick off, and banners that show who wrote.

- **A quieter Jefi.** Lighter rows, softer text and borders, borderless cards, and a violet accent kept for what matters: Send and unread mail. Jefi opens in Liquid Glass, with your desktop showing softly through the window; Settings → Appearance → Style switches back to the solid look. Calendar events are glass cards with a dot in their calendar's colour, and folder counts past 999 shorten to 1.2k.
- **Notes that read like notes.** A note opens rendered, and its checklist items tick in place and save at once. Edit (or Enter) brings back the Markdown with a formatting toolbar: headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, lists, checklists, quotes and links, and ⇧⌘L starts a checklist item as in Apple Notes. Done (or Esc) goes back to reading.
- **A tidier notes list.** Notes are grouped Pinned, Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days, Previous 30 days and Earlier, and each shows how far along its checklist is. The footer counts words and links.
- **Who wrote, at a glance.** New-mail banners show the sender's photo, or their initials in a colour that's always theirs; mail from several people at once shows an envelope. Task reminders show a checklist and event reminders a calendar.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
