# Jefi 0.7.1

Other apps on your Mac can now reach Jefi: through links, from the command line, and, if you allow it, from AI apps.

- **jefi:// links.** A link can open Jefi on a new message with the recipients, subject and body filled in, a mail search, a day in the calendar, an event, the event editor (from a sentence like "Lunch with Nora Friday 1pm"), a note or a new note, or a section of Settings. Links open things for you to finish; a compose link never sends. Jefi doesn't claim mailto: links, so your default mail app stays your choice.
- **A command line.** `/Applications/Jefi.app/Contents/MacOS/Jefi cli` reads your accounts, unread counts, search results, inbox, a conversation as plain text, today's events, your agenda, invitations waiting for a reply, and notes. Add `--json` for scripts. It only reads, and Settings → Integrations shows the exact command.
- **Jefi for AI apps (MCP).** Turn on **Settings → Integrations → Allow AI apps to read Jefi (MCP)** and AI apps such as Claude Desktop, Codex and Tinycast can search and read your mail, calendar and notes. Settings shows the setup for each. When an AI app wants to write something, Jefi opens a draft, an event or a note, filled in, for you to review; nothing is sent, saved or deleted without you. It's off until you turn it on, and it runs only on your Mac, with no network port.
- **Coming soon: Raycast and Tinycast.** An extension that searches your mail, shows today's agenda and adds events from Raycast or Tinycast is on its way.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
