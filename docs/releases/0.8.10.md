# Jefi 0.8.10

Small things, tidied up.

- **Label counts that add up.** A label's unread count and its Mark all as read now leave out Spam and Trash, just as the label's view does.
- **Commands you can read.** Setup commands in Settings → Integrations wrap inside their boxes instead of running off the edge, and Copy still copies them exactly.
- **Tasks that keep your place.** J and K carry on from a task you've just completed instead of jumping back to the top, and a project shows the same number of tasks in the sidebar and in its header.
- **One time per invitation.** Invitations written in UTC no longer show a second, identical time line; a different organizer time zone is still shown.
- **No more waiting on Settings.** The notification permission row shows its status straight away instead of "Checking with macOS…" every time you scroll to it.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
