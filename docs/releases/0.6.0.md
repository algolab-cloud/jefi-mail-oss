# Jefi 0.6.0

Mail arrives the moment it's sent, and Jefi stays quiet while it keeps up.

- **New mail, right away.** Jefi listens for new mail and shows it within a second or two, with its preview ready. Coming back from sleep catches up at once instead of waiting for the next check.
- **A calmer sidebar.** Background syncs no longer spin in the corner or flash through folder names. The status reads Up to date, the time of the last check is in its tooltip, and a sync problem is only reported when it keeps happening. Syncs you start yourself still show their progress.
- **Your mail, all there.** An interrupted sync, such as a dropped connection, sleep, or a server error, could make Jefi treat a folder as empty and remove its messages from the copy on your Mac. That no longer happens, and the next sync downloads anything that went missing, newest first. Nothing on your mail server was ever affected. Large mailboxes can take a while to fill in; message previews follow.
- **Lighter downloads.** Message text downloads in the background, newest first, and steps aside whenever new mail arrives or you open a conversation. Attachments download only when you open them.
- **Less work per sync.** Folders that haven't changed since the last check are skipped, each account keeps one connection open instead of signing in for every check, and servers that support it report only what changed.
- **Tidier notifications.** Several messages arriving together become one notification instead of a stack.
- **Cleaner account removal.** Removing an account now removes its downloaded mail from your Mac too, and mail left behind by earlier removals is cleaned up on launch.

The first launch after updating may spend some time bringing older messages back into place. Jefi stays usable while it works.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running **macOS 13 or later**.
